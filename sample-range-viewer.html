Built to the drawing

This round

  • Side vans 25% larger, .377 to .471, inner edge still on the content column. One consequence to know about: at that size the outer edge lands at 11.97 against an 11.88rem half-window, so roughly 7px of the far end sits off screen. Holding the inner edge on the column is the rule you picked, so that is what gives — pulling it in to clear the window would push the van 7px over the column instead.
  • A full-bleed band under the features, carrying the buttons and the disclaimer. #FFF2DC — the site’s light panel, the one behind the Legendary Service slides and the ambassador tips, and the only light yellow in the system. Tell me if you meant a different one.
  • The buttons had to come out of the per-model panel to sit in the band, so they are now their own cross-faded stack — one row per model, same timing as everything else.
  • The section carries no bottom padding now. The band ends it, the way the orange stat band runs straight into the Our-range photo above.
  • Carousel circles removed. The arrows in the margins, a click on either side van, the arrow keys and a swipe all still work.
  • Labels shortened to Explore and Brochure. The buttons were already .btn-solid’s exact spec — 0.30rem Aviano Black, 0.24/0.34 padding, 0.78rem tall, the same as Meet Solara. It was the label length making the pair read as oversized, not the size.
  • Height is now 10.89rem = 0.458 of the window width, comfortably inside the half-width rule.

The gaps, and where each one comes from

  • Four values, no others. 1.20rem the section’s own padding, which is what .solara, .dream and .lux use. 0.72rem a major break, taken from .range-list’s gap after its lede. 0.40rem the standard gap, taken from .solara-lede under its title. 0.06rem a tight pair.
  • It replaced a spread of 0.18 / 0.26 / 0.30 / 0.34 / 0.38 / 0.40 — six near-identical values that read as drift rather than rhythm.
  • Worth knowing: the old .range section ran 0.95 top and 1.07 bottom, which is itself off the house 1.20. Removing it takes that inconsistency with it.
