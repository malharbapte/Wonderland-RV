Pick the gap at the top

  • Nothing resizes. The vans stay at .471 and the stage stays 5.10 — only how far out a neighbour sits changes.
  • Why they ended up on the margins: last round the rule was hold the neighbour’s inner edge on the content column, and the gap was whatever fell out of that — which turned out to be 4.03rem. That is the wrong way round. The offset is now driven by the gap you want, and the position falls out of it.
  • The arithmetic, if useful: the centre van’s half-width is 4.09rem and a neighbour’s is 1.93rem, so for a gap of g the offset is 6.02 + g, which is --nb as a percentage of the 16.24 slot.
  • Tight (0.30rem) is loaded by default. Touching and Overlapping are there because at this size they may well read better than any true gap — the neighbours start to behave like a stack rather than three separate vans.

The gaps, and where each one comes from

  • Four values, no others. 1.20rem the section’s own padding, which is what .solara, .dream and .lux use. 0.72rem a major break, taken from .range-list’s gap after its lede. 0.40rem the standard gap, taken from .solara-lede under its title. 0.06rem a tight pair.
  • It replaced a spread of 0.18 / 0.26 / 0.30 / 0.34 / 0.38 / 0.40 — six near-identical values that read as drift rather than rhythm.
  • Worth knowing: the old .range section ran 0.95 top and 1.07 bottom, which is itself off the house 1.20. Removing it takes that inconsistency with it.
