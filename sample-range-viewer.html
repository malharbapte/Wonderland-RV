What else could sit in here

Worth adding — needs a number from you

  • Tare and ATM. The first question a buyer actually has is can my car tow it, and nothing else on the page answers it. Your model pages publish suspension load capacity (3.0T / 3.7T / 4.5T) but not tare or ATM, so I can’t fill it truthfully. If you give me the four pairs this is the single most useful cell on the strip.
  • Sleeps. “Sleeps 2–6” sorts couples from families in one glance and is the other half of how people self-select.
  • Length range and layout count. Lotus gives this line prominence (“sizes ranging from 18’9” to 24’6””). Your homepage says 17’05”–24’ and 30+ layouts across the whole range; per model it isn’t published, and the Amaroo page implies 17’09” rather than 17’05”, so those two need reconciling before either goes on screen.

Can be built from what already exists

  • An award ribbon. Solara won the 2026 Judge’s Choice Queen of Hearts — already on your homepage. A small ribbon on its stage is free credibility, and it only appears on the one model, which is the point.
  • Construction, as the fourth cell. Solara is frameless composite sandwich panel; the other three are composite wrap-around with SPR aluminium side frames. That is a real, sourced difference and it is the reason Solara costs what it does.
  • Brochure download. You have a per-model brochure thumbnail and PDF already. A second, quieter link beside Explore converts people who aren’t ready to enquire.
  • More angles, cheaply. The series I took the 3/4 from has 7–8 frames per model. Not enough for a true 360 (that wants 24–36, evenly spaced — these include front-on and rear-on, so a spin would stutter), but plenty for a small angle-picker: front 3/4, side, rear 3/4, front, rear. Cheapest version of the 360 idea by a mile.

I’d leave out

  • A fifth and sixth feature. Four cells across 13.6rem is already tight at 1440. Anything more and the strip stops being scannable, which is the only thing it is for.
  • Layout thumbnails here. They need room to be legible and they belong on the model page, not in a stage that has to stay one screen tall.
