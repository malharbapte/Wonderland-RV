Built to the drawing

This round

  • Name and price are one tier at one size — both 0.46rem, on the page margins. Tagline and From are the other tier, both 0.20rem. Two sizes in that block instead of four.
  • The vans overflow. The stage is 6.40rem tall, which puts the viewed van at 10.27rem across — inside the 16.24rem content column. The neighbours sit at 59%, so their outer edges land 12.56rem from centre against an 11.88rem half-window: it is the window that cuts them, not a container.
  • Features are centred, with the icon still beside its title as you drew it — the pair is what gets centred, not the icon alone.
  • The buttons are .btn-solid’s metrics exactly. They were already the right font and padding; a min-width I had put on them is what made them read as outsized. It is gone.
  • Snap is gone. Glide only, and the switch bar with it.

The gaps, and where each one comes from

  • Four values, no others. 1.20rem the section’s own padding, which is what .solara, .dream and .lux use. 0.72rem a major break, taken from .range-list’s gap after its lede. 0.40rem the standard gap, taken from .solara-lede under its title. 0.06rem a tight pair.
  • It replaced a spread of 0.18 / 0.26 / 0.30 / 0.34 / 0.38 / 0.40 — six near-identical values that read as drift rather than rhythm.
  • Worth knowing: the old .range section ran 0.95 top and 1.07 bottom, which is itself off the house 1.20. Removing it takes that inconsistency with it.
