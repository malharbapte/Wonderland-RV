Range

Four models and loads of customisations mean you can Wonderland RV your way.

Figures shown are for the standard build. Every model can be specified further — options change capacities and price.

What changed

The homepage’s language, not its own

  • The section now states itself the way every other homepage section does: a left-aligned Range at --t-heading with the lede under it, on the site’s own --section-gap rhythm. The centred small-caps “OUR RANGE” was a Lotus habit, not ours.
  • The call to action is .btn-solid itself — the class the Solara block and the rest of the homepage already use — rather than a lookalike. Nothing here restyles a shared class, so there is no way for this section to change a button anywhere else.
  • Dot colour moved from --orange to --orange-deep, which is what the homepage uses for accents on a light ground.

Hierarchy, as you set it

  • Name, then price, then tagline — in that order down the page and in that order of size. Price used to sit below the tagline and that put the quietest thing in the loudest position.
  • Inside a detail: icon, then title, then the figure and its note at one size. That is a real inversion — the figure used to be the biggest thing in the cell. It now reads as a labelled group rather than as four competing numbers.

Badges

  • One shape, one size, no icons on any of them. A star on one chip and nothing on the next was itself an inconsistency, and at that size the glyph read as grit. Only the fill separates them now: filled is an award, outlined is a statement about the van.
  • XTR’s Flagship is outlined, same as Solara’s composite mark. Solara’s Queen of Hearts keeps the fill, because it is the only one of the three that was given by someone else.

Still open

  • The brochure link points nowhere. Four PDF URLs and it is done.
  • Phone is untouched and gated — the section is display:none below 900 and the existing range carousel still runs there.
