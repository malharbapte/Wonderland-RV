Built to the drawing

This round

  • The heading is gone. The full-bleed “Our range” band directly above already names this section, so a second Range under it was saying it twice. The lede opens the section now and runs on one line — the max-width that used to break it was sized for a column that no longer applies.
  • The arrows hang in the page margins, level with the model name on the left and the price on the right. --page-margin is 3.76, half of it is 1.88, and the arrow’s own half-width is 0.28 — so each lands on the centre line of its margin, clear of the content column. The dot row keeps the dots and nothing else.
  • Icons up 20%, 0.44 to 0.53rem.
  • Side vans down 35%, .58 to .377. One thing that came with it: at that size their outer edges fell inside the window and the overflow would have quietly stopped happening, so they are pushed from 59% out to 62% — half-width 1.94rem at an offset of 10.07 puts the edge at 12.01 against an 11.88rem half-window. Still clipped by the window, as you picked.

The gaps, and where each one comes from

  • Four values, no others. 1.20rem the section’s own padding, which is what .solara, .dream and .lux use. 0.72rem a major break, taken from .range-list’s gap after its lede. 0.40rem the standard gap, taken from .solara-lede under its title. 0.06rem a tight pair.
  • It replaced a spread of 0.18 / 0.26 / 0.30 / 0.34 / 0.38 / 0.40 — six near-identical values that read as drift rather than rhythm.
  • Worth knowing: the old .range section ran 0.95 top and 1.07 bottom, which is itself off the house 1.20. Removing it takes that inconsistency with it.
