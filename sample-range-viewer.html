This round

  • Touching is loaded, and the viewed van is 20% bigger — the stage went 5.10 to 6.12rem, so it is 9.82rem across rather than 8.18.
  • The neighbours did not grow with it. Their scale is a fraction of the slot, so a 20% taller stage would have made them 20% bigger too. .471 / 1.2 = .393 keeps them exactly the size they were. Any future change to the stage height has to be divided through there as well or they drift.
  • The gap switcher is still at the top and the numbers are recalculated for the new centre van: the offset for a gap of g is now 6.84 + g, and 0 gives 42.1%.

One thing this costs

  • At 5.10 the section was 11.29rem = 0.475 of the window width, inside the half-width rule. At 6.12 it is 12.31rem = 0.518, about 995px at 1920 — that wants a window around 1100 tall to show in full, so on a 1080 screen the last of the cream band will sit just under the fold.
  • Getting back under the rule means taking 0.43rem off somewhere else. The two places with any give are the band’s padding and the gap at the top. Say the word and I will take it from either — or leave it, if a van that size is worth the scroll.

The gaps, and where each one comes from

  • Four values, no others. 1.20rem the section’s own padding, which is what .solara, .dream and .lux use. 0.72rem a major break, taken from .range-list’s gap after its lede. 0.40rem the standard gap, taken from .solara-lede under its title. 0.06rem a tight pair.
  • It replaced a spread of 0.18 / 0.26 / 0.30 / 0.34 / 0.38 / 0.40 — six near-identical values that read as drift rather than rhythm.
  • Worth knowing: the old .range section ran 0.95 top and 1.07 bottom, which is itself off the house 1.20. Removing it takes that inconsistency with it.
