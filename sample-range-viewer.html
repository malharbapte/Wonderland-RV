Three things to settle

  • Dark or light. Lotus's stage is black and it is a lot of why it looks the way it does — but every cut-out on this site currently sits on white or cream, so black is a break from the house language. The switch at the top shows both. Your call.
  • Which four features. On your own four, water reads 290L on three of four models and brakes read 12″ drum on three of four — half the strip says the same thing three times, which makes the models look more alike than they are. "Differentiating" swaps water and brakes for battery and inverter, which separate all four cleanly. Lotus uses solar / battery / water / inverter for exactly this reason.
  • How hard the step lands. "Snap" is the Lotus timing you were unsure about; "Glide" is the same move 70% slower with a softer curve.
