Where this stands

Everything shown is sourced

  • Price and all four features are quoted from wonderlandrv.com.au/range/<model>/. Nothing on screen is invented.
  • Tare and ATM are out. Both move with the options on a given build, and they are layout-specific rather than per model — an Amaroo 17’09” and an Amaroo 24’ do not share a tare. A figure a buyer would act on and then find wrong is worse than no figure.

The arrangement question

  • Stage keeps the big wordmark and the symmetry. It is the more striking of the two and the closer to Lotus.
  • Split puts the van left and the writing right, which scans faster and costs the wordmark.
  • My read: Stage for the homepage, because the homepage’s job is to make someone want a van, not to let them audit one. Split is the better model page pattern.
