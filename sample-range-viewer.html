How the artboard was mapped

  • Your artboard is 1920 wide; this site’s is 2376, where 1rem is 100 artboard px. The heading sits at x = 306.91, and 306.91 × 2376/1920 = 380 — the site’s 3.76rem page margin. So the drawing maps onto our scale at 1 SVG px = 0.012375rem, and every size and position below is your own measurement put through that. At a 1920-wide window this is the drawing at 1:1.
  • The plate is 941.42 × 481.56 and dead centre. The two neighbours are the same plate at 49.3% on the same axis — and your left one starts at x = −4.51, so they are meant to be cut by the window, which is what they do.
  • The buttons are centred on the page, not on the plate — their pair straddles x = 960 exactly. The name and price are the opposite: both sit on the plate’s edges, not the page margin.

Decisions I made, flag any you want back

  • Feature photos are real. The grey plates are filled with your own exterior shots from wonderlandrv.com.au, cropped to the drawing’s 941:482 around the van. Switch to Cut-out at the top to see the transparent 3/4 renders in the same slots instead.
  • Badges are all one pill — square left edge, round right end, orange, white type — exactly as your “Full-composite” chip is drawn. Solara carries two, XTR carries Flagship.
  • Icons are ink, not orange. In your drawing everything is black except the values, the price and the buttons; making the icons orange too would have spread the accent over half the strip.
  • Brakes carry no note. You wrote the cell as one line, “12”DRUM”, with nothing under it, so the note line is simply absent on that cell rather than padded out.
  • The heading is 0.65rem and the lede 0.35rem, which is what your 65.25 and 34.59 come to. Both are larger than the homepage’s standard section heading, so this section will read louder than its neighbours. Deliberate or not, it is your drawing — say the word and I will bring it to --t-heading.
  • Arrows and dots are not in the drawing. They are under the buttons on the same centre line, and every interaction from the last round is unchanged.
  • “REDRAC” in the drawing is set as REDARC here, which is the brand.
