Built to the drawing

This round

  • The line moved onto the band, under “Our range”, and lost its second half. The band and the viewer now read as one section rather than as a photo followed by a carousel. Scoped to .band--range so .band--why, which uses the same classes, does not move.
  • Spelling: set as customisations, which is how it reads everywhere else on the site. Say the word if you want the z.
  • It fits one screen now. At 1920 wide a rem is 80.8px and the section adds to 12.19rem = 985px, which clears a 1080 window with its header. It was 1180px, which is why you had to scroll. The stage came down from 6.40 to 5.40, so the viewed van is 8.66rem across rather than 10.27.
  • The top padding is the standard 0.40, not the section’s 1.20. The band above is the top of this section; 1.20 between them made them two things.
  • The side vans moved out again, 62% to 65%. That number has to change every time the stage height does, or their outer edges fall inside the window and the overflow quietly stops happening.

The gaps, and where each one comes from

  • Four values, no others. 1.20rem the section’s own padding, which is what .solara, .dream and .lux use. 0.72rem a major break, taken from .range-list’s gap after its lede. 0.40rem the standard gap, taken from .solara-lede under its title. 0.06rem a tight pair.
  • It replaced a spread of 0.18 / 0.26 / 0.30 / 0.34 / 0.38 / 0.40 — six near-identical values that read as drift rather than rhythm.
  • Worth knowing: the old .range section ran 0.95 top and 1.07 bottom, which is itself off the house 1.20. Removing it takes that inconsistency with it.
