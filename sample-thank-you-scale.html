How a Wonderland RV is built

01

Chassis & suspension

Every van starts with its chassis and Cruisemaster suspension, fitted in our own factory in Campbellfield.

02

Frame & panels

Insulated aluminium side frames, SPR (self-piercing riveted), with a one-piece composite front, roof and back wall on the XTR, Hornet and Amaroo. Solara goes frameless composite.

03

Cabinetry & fit-out

Cabinetry is CNC cut for a precise, interlocking fit, then the wiring and interior are fitted in the same factory.
