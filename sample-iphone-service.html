iPhone — Legendary Service, revised

L1’s inset card with L3’s order — photo first, review beneath — and the mark moved onto the seam between them.

The markAnchored to the bottom of the photo and pulled up by half its own height, so exactly half of it sits on the photograph and half on the cream. It holds that split at any width, because it is measured from the join rather than from a fixed distance.
What it buysThe quote no longer has to be indented around it, so the review gets the full 292 of measure instead of the 244 it had when the mark sat beside the text.
