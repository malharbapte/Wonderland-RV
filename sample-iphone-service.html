iPhone — Legendary Service

This section is shared with the contact page, so whatever we settle here lands on both. Four reviews, each a quote and a photo.

PaddingThe section's top padding is 8.8px on the phone. The mobile rule asks for 48, but a desktop rule in homepage.css loads after it and wins, so the heading sits almost against the band above it.
A fifth slideThe desktop loop appends a clone of the first review so it can wrap without a visible rewind. On the phone that clone is just a swipeable duplicate: you reach Julian Randall twice. It needs the same treatment Our range got — loop properly, or drop the clone.
No affordanceThe arrows are hidden here and nothing replaces them, so there is no sign the four scroll. All three below add dots and a counter.
The markHidden on the phone. All three bring it back at the phone scale we settled on the pull quote — 3.1301× the text, hanging 12 into the margin.
