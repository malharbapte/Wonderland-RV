Why it reads bland, and what each one does about it

Three things are causing it, and they are worth separating because they have different fixes. The vans float — they are cut-outs on flat white with nothing underneath, so they read as pasted on rather than standing anywhere. The section is one tone — white, with colour only on four values and a price, against a cream band that arrives at the very bottom. And the step does not reward watching — a straight crossfade in and out, nothing arrives, nothing settles.

Every one of these includes the shadow

Not a style choice. It is the single biggest reason the section looks flat, and it costs nothing — a soft ellipse under each van, weaker under the neighbours. Toggle As it is and back to see how much of the problem it was on its own.

What each adds on top

  • T1 Grounded — the shadow plus a horizon: a wash across the lower part of the stage so the vans stand on a surface. The smallest change here and the one that is hardest to argue with.
  • T2 Two tone — the section takes the site’s cream and the stage stays white as a panel inside it. The band below is already #FFF2DC, so you get three warm tones stacked instead of one flat white, and the stage becomes something the vans are on.
  • T3 Ghost wordmark — the model name again, very large and very pale, behind the van. Lotus’s own move and the most distinctive of the four. It adds no information: it is the name already sitting under the stage. The risk is that it is the furthest from the rest of this site, which has nothing like it anywhere.
  • T4 It moves — the step stops being a straight crossfade. The name and price lift in, the four features rise in sequence behind them, and the neighbours drift as they go. Costs nothing visually when the section is at rest, which is most of the time.

If you want my read

T2 with T4’s motion. Two tone fixes the flatness at rest, which is the state the section is in whenever nobody is touching it; the staged entrance fixes the part you only see once you engage. T3 is the most striking of the four but it is a new idea on a site that does not use one anywhere else — worth doing deliberately, not by drift. Say the word and I will combine any two of these.
