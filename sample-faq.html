FAQs — three layouts

Sitting on home.html, between the contact band and the footer. That is the only page the brief fits: on the contact page the form is near the top, and the section before the footer there is Where to next. The hatched strips in each frame mark the seam either side — they are a sample device, not design. Desktop only: the whole section is hidden below 900px in all three.

Two things to know before you judge the copy
  • Wonderland has no FAQ page. I checked the sitemap — 61 pages, none of them FAQs — so there was nothing to lift. Rather than invent answers, every one of these five is grounded in something the business already publishes: the Campbellfield address and factory tours, the couples/family custom build, Cruisemaster, Goodrich and Dometic, the seven dealer locations, and the 35mm composite panels line from your own Things You Can't See section.
  • The two questions people actually ask most are deliberately missing: how long a build takes, and what the warranty covers. I have no source for either and they are not the sort of thing to guess at in public. Give me the answers and they go in.
Flagging a design-language question, as you asked me to

This site has no desktop accordion. All three that exist — .why-acc, .dream-acc and .foot-acc — live inside @media (max-width: 900px) and were built for the phone only. Option 1 would introduce one. The one reveal pattern this site already ships on desktop is the travel-tips panel on the Wonderlander pages, which is what Option 3 is built from. Option 2 introduces nothing at all. That is the real choice here; the rest is taste.

Option 1Accordion, full width

558px tallnew desktop pattern

The conventional FAQ. Questions stacked on hairlines, a plus that rotates into a minus, one open at a time, height eased on the same curve as the Budget field and the phone accordions. Opens on the first question so the section is never a row of closed doors. The pattern is new to this breakpoint, though the movement is not.

Option 2Two columns, nothing hidden

introduces nothing532px tall

No interaction whatsoever. Both columns on the site's own 7.88 / 7.88 grid, every answer on screen, question in Aviano and answer in Gordita beneath it. Nothing new to the language, no state to get wrong, and every word visible to a search engine without a click. It reads as part of the page rather than as a widget sitting on it. The trade is that it cannot grow: at ten questions this becomes a wall.

Option 3Question list and answer panel

already ships on desktop474px — shortest, and fixed

The travel-tips mechanic from the Wonderlander pages: questions down the left, the answer crossfading into a cream panel on the right. All five answers share one grid cell, so the panel is always as tall as the longest of them and the section never changes height while someone reads — nothing below it ever moves. The cream is #FFF2DC, the same panel colour those pages already use and the same one the Budget disclaimer picked up.

It is the shortest of the three and the only one whose height is constant. It also scales best: ten questions make the left column longer without touching the panel.
