Wonderlanders cards — on the phone

Each panel below is a real 375px-wide phone viewport, so these are the actual phone layouts rather than a scaled-down desktop. Scroll or drag inside a panel to try it. Desktop is untouched by all three — every rule here lives inside the phone block.

1 · As it is today

One card per row, capped at 320px and centred. Every Wonderlander is reachable by scrolling straight down, but six full-width cards make the section very tall, and the bios sit open the whole way so there is a lot to scroll past.

Scrolls vertically.

2 · Two rows deep, sliding — the one you asked for

Cards pair up vertically and the pairs slide sideways, two rows always in view. Cuts the section to roughly a third of its height and keeps the whole roster within a couple of swipes. Runs endlessly, so it never dead-ends at Kiwi Van Life.

Drag sideways — it keeps going.

3 · One row, big cards

A single sliding row at 78% of the screen width, so one card is the focus and the next peeks in. The photography is far bigger than either other option; the cost is that it takes six swipes to see everyone. Also endless.

Drag sideways — it keeps going.
