Log-lines, stated rather than asked

No opening What, Which, How or When, and nothing that resolves into a question. The openers vary on purpose — six videos in one deck all starting “Discover” would read as a template, which is a worse fault than the questions were.

1   Queen of hearts

shipped What makes the judges at Caravan of the Year pick one van, out of a whole field, as the one they would take home themselves.

assertive Caravan World’s judges invented an award so they could name the one van they would take home themselves. It went to a twenty-foot-six couples van.

2   How is a Wonderland built.

shipped Two caravans can sit side by side on a showroom floor, look the same and cost thousands apart. This is where that money goes.

assertive Discover where the money goes in a caravan that costs thousands more than the one beside it — in the parts you stop being able to see once the walls go on.

3   A caravan built for two.

shipped What a caravan looks like when it is built for two people to live in it rather than just sleep in it.

assertive Step inside a layout built for two people to live in rather than just sleep in, where the rear lounge is the room that decides it.

4   Where the kids sleep.

shipped Why three bunks beat four the moment a child turns twelve, and what changes when you stop counting beds and start measuring them.

assertive See why three bunks beat four the moment a child turns twelve, and what changes when you stop counting beds and start measuring them.

5   Stay out longer.

shipped How much power and water it takes to stay out for weeks instead of nights — and what carrying it costs you in space.

assertive Find out what it takes to stay out for weeks instead of nights — forty-eight volts, a hundred and seventy litres, and the space they cost.

6   It won all three.

shipped Three Caravan of the Year titles in one night — best overall, best family, best innovation. The van and the spec that took them.

assertive Meet the family van that took three Caravan of the Year titles in one night: best overall, best family and best innovation.
