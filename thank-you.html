Frequently asked questions

One of our experts will reach out to you within 24 hours on business days. They will listen to how you plan to use your van, then help bring your dream to life.

Every one of them in Australia, at our own factory at 46 Lara Way, Campbellfield, Victoria. You are welcome to come and watch it happen — book a factory tour and we will walk you through a van on the line.

That is rather the point. Because no two travellers are the same, we believe no two caravans should be either. Start from a couples or a family layout and we will build it around how you actually travel.

35mm composite panels, bonded and bolted into an aluminium frame. It is the part of the van you will never see, and it is the part that decides how it holds up.

Only names we are prepared to stand behind — Cruisemaster suspension, Goodrich tyres and Dometic appliances among them. We would rather argue about the price than about the parts.

Through our dealers in Melbourne, Sydney, Port Stephens, Brisbane, Mackay, Perth and Tasmania — or at the factory in Campbellfield, which is where the vans are actually built.
