Brand logos — uniform size

Every mark has been trimmed to its ink and re-centred on an identical 520×170 canvas, so they all render at the same scale rather than each finding its own size. Hover to check the colour-up.

On the two-in-one placeholder you spotted: that is from slicing the greyscale strip, and it is not fixable by tuning. The gaps inside a wordmark and the gaps between two logos overlap in that image, so no single threshold separates them — three of the 21 slices held two marks each. These individual files replace that approach entirely.
