Why the plan looked small: it was not a design choice. .amb-plan keeps width: 13.72rem from the desktop scale, and rem flattens to 16px below 901 — so the box was 220px wide, and inside a 200px-tall box set to contain the artwork was painting at just 220 × 93 on a 375 screen, letterboxed above and below. All three options size it from the artwork’s own ratio instead. The dropdown sub-headings and the wider gap before the pink box are in all three too — those are fixes, not choices.
