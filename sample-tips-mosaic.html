Travel tips: Option C

Desktop only. Each page now shows only its own family’s photos. Any tile without a photo is plain orange, spread out so the orange tiles don’t sit side by side.

A limit worth knowing. This mosaic has 14 tiles, and at most 6 of them can be orange without two touching. So the rule fully holds once a page has 8 or more photos. Below that, the orange is placed so that as few tiles touch as possible. The line under the frame tells you how many pairs touch.

The shuffle. Photos trade places every 3 seconds, and orange tiles move around with them. A move is only allowed if it doesn’t make the orange more bunched up than it already is. Once a page has more than 14 photos, new ones rotate in and anything that just left rests for 8 changes before it can come back.

The questions in each frame are that page’s own, read from its live file.
