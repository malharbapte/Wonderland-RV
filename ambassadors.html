Real families on the road in Wonderland RV caravans

The Feel Good Family planned a two-year lap that turned into seven years, and The Kiwi Van Life sold their house in seven days, then made van life their long-term lifestyle. Questions about the Wonderlanders

What is a Wonderlander?

A Wonderlander is a Wonderland RV owner, part of the community of people who travel in Wonderland RV caravans. The families on these pages are Wonderlanders who share their vans and their tips for life on the road, from caravan storage ideas to their favourite camping recipes.

How do families live in a caravan full time with kids?

Several of the Wonderlander families homeschool on the road and work from wherever they are parked. The Feel Good Family run their business as they travel, Sandy Van Travels work remotely on their photography, and both rely on Starlink to stay connected. The Kiwi Van Life homeschool too, and say the kids learn so much more out there.

Where are Wonderland RV caravans built?

Wonderland RV caravans are built in Campbellfield, Melbourne, where the family-owned company has its factory and showroom. Every van is Australian made and built to order, and Wonderland RV has been building off-road caravans since 2014. Each one comes with a 3 year factory-backed warranty and premium aftersales service.

How long does a big lap of Australia take?

There’s no set length for a big lap of Australia, and for two of the Wonderlander families the plan changed on the road. The Feel Good Family set out on a two-year lap in 2019 and are still going seven years later. The Kiwi Van Life planned a year, then made it their long-term lifestyle after fourteen months.

What caravans do the Wonderlanders travel in?

The Wonderlanders travel in all kinds of Wonderland RV caravans. The Feel Good Family have a Solara, The Kiwi Van Life a Hornet, the Wheatland Wanderers and Livin In Sanity each have an XTR, and Sandy Van Travels have a three-bunk layout for their family of five. Compare the Wonderland RV range.
