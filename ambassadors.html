Meet our
Wonderlanders

Voices to our inspiration

Our Wonderlanders are the faces behind the journeys.

We’re proud to introduce you to our esteemed Wonderlanders. A group of individuals who embody the spirit of wanderlust. From seasoned travellers to outdoor experts, each Wonderlander brings a unique perspective to our community. But they all have in common the love for off-grid adventures and life-changing experiences!

Lost Tribe Adventures

Exploring breathtaking landscapes since 2021, Lost Tribe Adventures have turned off-road caravanning into a lifestyle. Aaron and Steph are creating lasting memories with their two beautiful children, Wes and Georgi.

Wheatland Wanderers

The Wheatland Wanderers are Cam, Sharyn, and their sons Jye and Cruz. They started out their journey inside of tents, pop-up caravans, and expanders.

Few Laps Around the Sun

Meet Zane and Casey, the adventurous duo behind “Few Laps Around the Sun,” a captivating journey through the heart of Australia.

Sandy Van Travels

Jess and Doug sold everything in Brisbane to travel Australia full-time with their three kids, documenting the journey through photography, video and honest storytelling.

Livin In Sanity

Since February 2023, this beautiful family has been full-time travelling, capturing awesome moments and sharing epic memories.
