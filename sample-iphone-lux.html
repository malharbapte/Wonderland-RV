iPhone — heading against the quote

B1 in all three, unchanged: mark at the desktop ratio, photos at their native 1.691 with nothing cropped. The only difference is the heading size and, in the third, the quote size. Under each phone are the three longest headings on the site set at that size, so the cost of going bigger is visible rather than a surprise later.

Nowheading 26, quote 17 — a ratio of 1.53. The quote is only a little smaller, and it is orange, italic and four lines deep, so it carries more weight than the heading despite being the smaller type.
