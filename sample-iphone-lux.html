iPhone — Luxury off-road, option B

Option B in three versions. They differ in two things only: how big the quote mark is and how the text clears it, and how much of each photo is kept. Everything else is identical — copy first, then a swipeable gallery with a peek, dots and a counter.

The two corrections

The markOn the desktop it is 1.1968rem against 0.325rem text — 3.6825× the font size — eased right by 0.15rem, or 0.4615×. Both ratios are carried over and multiplied by the phone’s 17, so it sits exactly as it does on the desktop. Because it hangs up and to the left of the first letter, the quote has to be indented or the mark falls off the screen. That is the difference between the three below.
The photosAll five are 2200×1301, aspect 1.691. The old sample put them in a 1.5 box with no object-position, so the sides were trimmed by default rather than by choice. Each version now states the frame, and states object-position: 50% 50% so whatever is cropped is taken evenly off both edges and the middle band always survives.

The three versions

B1 — Faithful

Mark at the full desktop proportion (63px against 17px text). Text indented to 79 so the whole mark sits inside the margin. Photos at their native 1.691 — nothing is cropped at all. Measure drops to 287.
