iPhone — Luxury off-road caravans

Three ways this section could work on a phone. Each column is a real 390-wide render. Swipe the photos in A and B with a trackpad or by dragging.

What is actually wrong now

The carouselIt does scroll — 5 slides, 390 each — but nothing says so. No dots, no counter, no peek of the next photo, and the swipe can chain into the page because overscroll-behavior-x is not set. It reads as one stray full-bleed image.
The quoteIts line breaks are hand-set for the desktop artboard. On a 342 column the lines run 117–189 wide — the shortest fills a third of the measure. That is the “messed up” text, and there are 46 hard breaks like it across the page.
The type10 distinct sizes are in use on the phone — 44, 32, 28, 20, 18, 16, 15, 14, 13, 12. No hierarchy, just a spread.
