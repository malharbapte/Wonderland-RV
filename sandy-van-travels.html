Sandy Van Travels

join them on their journey

We’re Jess and Doug, the family behind Sandy Van Travels, a full-time travelling family of five exploring Australia with our three children, Ellie and Arthur (our 7-year-old twins) and Harry (5).

After selling our home and most of what we owned in Brisbane, we custom built our first van in April 2024 and hit the road to create a life centred around family, adventure and experiences.

We now travel Australia full-time, working remotely, homeschooling our kids and documenting the journey through photography, video and honest storytelling.

Are Wonderland caravans built in Australia?

Yes. Every Wonderland RV caravan is designed, manufactured and finished at our factory in Melbourne. Our team:

  • Operates an integrated factory covering chassis, cabinetry, electrical, plumbing and fit-out trades
  • Has delivered more than 1300 caravans to owners across Australia
  • Builds to a standard specification plus options and upgrades, refined over years of feedback from owners on the road

Proudly Australian-made, Australian-owned, and serviced by the people who built it.
