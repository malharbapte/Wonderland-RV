The hover cursor

Hover each pair. Top still is bright, bottom still is dark — a translucent white circle behaves very differently on the two.

Drawn as a real CSS cursor, so the compositor paints it rather than the page: no lag on fast movement, nothing to run on scroll, and no JavaScript. 28–36px is the safe range — some platforms quietly ignore a cursor image much bigger than that and fall back, which is why none of these is larger. On a touch screen the whole thing is skipped; there is no cursor to draw.

1 — Plain

28px, white at 40%. Exactly what you asked for and nothing else. Reads beautifully on the darker stills; on a bright sky it is faint.

2 — With a hairline

32px, white at 45%, 1px dark edge at 16%. The same circle, given an edge so it does not disappear into a pale sky. The edge is barely there on a dark photo.

3 — Ring

36px, fill at 26% inside a white ring at 72%. Bigger and lighter — it reads as an outline more than a disc, and covers less of the photograph underneath.
