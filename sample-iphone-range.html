iPhone — Our range

Two things are actually broken here, and they are the same class of thing we have been finding: something sized in rem that collapsed, and something built for one width that does not survive another.

The iconsThe three feature icons are 0.62rem, which on the phone is 9.9px. They are absolutely positioned at left: 0 while the headings have their indent removed, so each icon is sitting underneath its own heading. This is the overlapping text you reported at the start.
The model gridIt is one baked PNG, 1016 wide, rendered at 0.337 scale. The four names and descriptors are pixels inside it, so they land around 9–10px equivalent — under the floor and unreadable — and the four chevrons are part of the picture, so nothing is tappable. I have cut the four vans out of it so the names can be real text again.

R1 — A row per model

Four tappable rows: van, name, descriptor, chevron. Names become real text at 16, descriptors at 12, and each row is a 60-plus target. The phone convention for a short list of things you pick between, and the only one where all four read at a glance.

Keeps the desktop’s 2×2 composition, but as four real cards rather than a picture of one. Names are real text at 12, descriptors at 11. Vans are half the width of R1, so they read as thumbnails.

R3 — Keep the PNG, full bleed

The least work: let the baked grid run to the screen edges instead of sitting inside the margin. Buys 48 of width, so the names go from about 9 to about 10. Still pixels, still under the floor, still nothing to tap. Shown so the do-nothing option is on the table honestly.
