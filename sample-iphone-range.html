iPhone — Our range, containers

H3 width kept. The vans now sit in rounded containers, the band is shown whole, and the heading has moved to the top of it.

Why the gaps were unevenYou had the cause exactly right. The four vans were cut from one PNG at their true relative sizes and centred on a shared frame, so a shorter van carries more air than a longer one. A stated container turns that from a mismatch into padding, which is why this works.
The corner radiusThere is no consistent curvature to match — the site currently uses ten different radii: 14, 10, 8, 6, 3, 0.353rem, 0.246rem, 0.18rem, 0.15rem, 0.146rem, 0.02rem. 10px is the most-used on the phone (the select, the textarea and the chips all sit there), so these boxes take 10 — and I would move the photo carousel in the block above from 6 to 10 to match. Worth settling as a token.
The bandWas forced to 240 tall with object-fit: cover, which cropped it. It is now shown at the photograph's own 2600×899, so nothing is cut, and the heading sits at the top.
