iPhone — Our range, horizontal

The four models as a horizontal scroll: van, name, caption. Same mechanics as the photo carousel in the Luxury off-road block above it — snap, contained overscroll, dots and a counter — so the page has one gesture rather than two.

The imagesTaken from the model pages on wonderlandrv.com.au, read only, nothing there touched. Solara, XTR, Hornet and Amaroo exteriors, each cropped from the centre to a common 3:2 and saved at 900×600 so they share one frame. The baked range-grid.png is not used.
The namesReal text now — 16 for the model, 12 for the caption — instead of pixels inside a PNG. Each card is a link.
The iconsBack to 36 and clear of their headings. They were 9.9 and sitting underneath them.

H1 — One up, generous peek

Card is the column less 82, so a good slice of the next van shows. Matches the photo carousel above almost exactly, which is the point — the same gesture, the same rhythm, twice on one page.
