Let’s Chat — the footer block before Subscribe

All three are live. Each shows the block in place: the line above it and the Subscribe heading below are greyed for context and are not part of the change.

What is measurably wrong today

  1. The social icons are 22px. Half the 44 a finger needs — the same fault already fixed on the video controls. Five bare glyphs on white with 24 between them.
  2. The three contact lines are undifferentiated. Phone, email and address are all 15px black text in one paragraph, but two are links and one is not. Nothing says which you can tap, and the phone number — the most useful thing in the footer — reads the same as the postcode.
  3. The block runs 226px for three lines, five icons and a badge.

L1Ruled rows

Phone, email and address become three ruled rows, each a 52 tap target with an orange glyph. Uses the exact rule and hairline of the link accordion directly below it, so the whole footer reads as one ruled stack. Socials become 44 circles.

—

L2Two buttons, one strip

Call and Email become a pair of outlined buttons — the two things anyone actually wants from a footer — with the address as a quiet line under them. Socials and the made-in badge share one row instead of stacking, which is where the height goes.

—

L3Cream panel

The whole block sits in the cream used by Legendary Service, which lifts the contact details out of a long white footer and gives them somewhere to live. Rows are 44 targets, socials sit on a tinted disc. The most visible, and the least like the rest of the footer.
