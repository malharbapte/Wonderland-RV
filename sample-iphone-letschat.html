On the gaps

With the row pinned to both edges the gap is not a number I get to pick — it is whatever is left over, divided evenly. Five 44 boxes in 327 leaves 107 across four gaps: 26.75 each, even by construction and about 0.6 of the glyph.

Bigger glyphs would tighten it further and smaller ones would open it up, so 44 is doing two jobs at once here: it is the smallest reliable tap target, and it is the size that lands the gap closest to the rhythm of the rules above.
