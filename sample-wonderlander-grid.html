Wonderlanders — card layout

Three ways to lay out the six cards. Hover any of them — the interaction is the same in all three and is not what is being chosen here. Drag or scroll the sliding ones sideways; both keep going rather than stopping at the last card.

1 · As it is today — static grid

Three across, two rows, all six in view at once and nothing moves. The whole roster is visible without any interaction, but it has a hard end and every card is the same size whatever the screen.

2 · Two rows deep, sliding — the one you asked for

Cards pair up vertically and the pairs march sideways — about four columns, so eight cards, sit in view at a desktop width, with more past the edge. grid-auto-flow: column is what stacks them in twos. The rail runs endlessly in both directions, so it never dead-ends the way the old strip did at Kiwi Van Life.
