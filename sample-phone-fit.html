Thank-you page on a phone: fit each section to one screen

Every frame is a 390 by 700 phone screen (844 less the header and browser bars). Everything inside works: tap, swipe (or drag with a mouse) and use the buttons. Copy and photos are the real ones from the site. On a phone, each frame fills the width.

Group 1

How a Wonderland RV is built

Three production steps in one screen: the photo and copy of each step only show when you ask for them.

Group 2

Long sections

The three tall sections folded into one screen each, two ways each. Reviews start at four, community at six, Load more adds the rest.

Group 3

Section accordions

The long sections (Reviews, Community, Awards, Build) become full-width bars. Closed bars are 64px, one opens at a time, the open one takes the rest of the screen.

Group 4

Knowing where you are

Each frame scrolls (it is the only group that does) and opens on the joint between Reviews and Build. Scroll to see how the page tells you where you are.
