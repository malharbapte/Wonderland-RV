Ready for Adventure — the form on iPhone

All three are live: tap the fields, tap the chips. The desktop form is untouched in every one of them — you said it looks perfect and it stays exactly as it is.

Measured on the page right now, at 375

  1. The field rhythm is broken. First Name to Last Name is 4px, Last Name to Email is 3px, then every gap after that is 26px. The top of the form reads as one squashed block and the bottom as a loose one.
  2. Touch targets are 40px. Apple’s floor is 44. Six of the seven controls are under it.
  3. The chips are 48, 68, 48, 68. Two of them carry a hard line break from the desktop layout, so they wrap to two lines at a width where they would fit on one. Uneven, and ~40px taller than it needs to be.
  4. The whole form runs 938px — you scroll it in three screenfuls.

C1Even it up

Nothing moves. One column, same order, same fields. Every gap becomes 22, every control becomes 48, and the chips lose their desktop line breaks so all four sit on one line at the same height. The smallest change that fixes all three faults.

—

C2Pair the short fields

Everything from C1, plus the fields that are short enough to share a line do: First and Last Name side by side, Model and Post Code side by side. Chips go two across. Cuts the form down by roughly a third without dropping anything.

—

C3Ask the question first

C2’s layout, reordered: the four chips come first, under a plain question, then the details. You say what you want before you type anything, which is the natural order to answer in and makes the first thing on screen a tap rather than a keyboard.
