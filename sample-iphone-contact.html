All three are live: tap a cell and the orange pill travels to it, squashing as it goes, exactly as it does on desktop. Same C2 layout in all three — the only difference is how the four options are drawn.

Corrected from the last sample

  • The pill is back. The phone block had turned .chips into a stacked grid of separate boxes, which left the sliding thumb nothing to slide along. It is one outlined container with four cells again, and one indicator that travels.
  • “Which model?” is gone — I invented that. The real field is State, and it is back with its chevron and its eight options.
  • “What can we help with” is gone. The real legend is sr-only by design — screen readers get it, the page does not show it.
  • The consent checkbox is back — I dropped it from the last sample entirely.
