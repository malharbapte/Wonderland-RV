The Wonderland Experience

Crafting your dream caravan, one detail at a time

Innovation is in our DNA

Since Wonderland RV opened shop in 2014, customisation and innovation are at the core of what we do. Our mission remained the same over the years: build bespoke caravans for caravanners who know what they want. We are attentive to their new ideas, we encourage our suppliers to push the boundaries of what’s possible, we listen to customer feedback and we test the caravans ourselves and always come back with a better way to do things.

Because no two travellers are
the same, we believe no two

Your Wonderland
customisation journey

Your dream vision

Envision your dream caravan. What kind of trips would you take? How many companions will travel with you? What features and amenities are essential for your comfort? Get inspired by fellow Wonderlanders, caravan shows, and ideas shared on social media.

The fundamental decisions

Select the model that best matches your needs and preferences, and chose from over 60 pre-defined layouts for couples and family configurations. Add your specifications, length, chassis, suspension, wheels, insulation, and more.

Make it your own

Personalise your caravan with a wide range of upgrades. Choose from electrical system, to cladding, privacy options and appliances. You can also select your preferred interior finishes, fabrics, and colours. We’re excited to create a space that’s truly your own!

Designing your caravan

Our creative team will take your inputs to come up with the perfect van design. They’ll prepare floor plans and elevations, and make sure all your requests work perfectly together so there isn’t any surprises at a later stage.

Watch your van coming to life

Our unmatched craftsmanship uses a unique jigsaw wall construction method for strength, durability, and precision. We build only with premium materials from reputable suppliers. Watch your van come to life with progress updates and images!

Your travel journey begins here

After a thorough inspection, we will hand over the keys to your new caravan. In our comprehensive handover session, we will explain how to use and maintain it. It will come with a 3-year factory-backed warranty and premium after-sales service for your peace of mind and satisfaction.

The customisation options

Layout

Select your desired caravan length, bed configuration, storage solutions, and seating arrangements, whether you are a couple or a family with kids.

Chassis

Pick your suspension and brakes, chassis finish, hitch, jockey wheel, stone guard, plus your choice of water tank, tunnel boot, and more.

Electrical

Tech, luxury and convenience are the goals of our offgrid electrical systems. Enjoy air conditioning, audio-visual media, connectivity, GPS, anti-theft tracking, lighting, and enough power for all the appliances you want to run to enhance your journey.

Internal Plan & Colours

Personalise your caravan space in order for it to be aesthetically pleasing to you by choosing from different colour themes of bathroom accessories, cupboard colours, handles, upholstery, flooring – plus decals and cladding colour for your exterior.

Plumbing & Appliances

Focus on functionality and comfort when you choose plumbing and appliance features for your home on wheels, like a heater, BBQ bayonet, fridge, hot water service, kitchen sink, and toilet.

External Plan

Customise your caravan’s external features – choose key options like entry door size, external kitchen, bike rack and a convenient picnic table – to suit your outdoor adventures and personal lifestyle.
