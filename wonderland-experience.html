The Wonderland Experience

Crafting your dream caravan, one detail at a time

Innovation is in our DNA

Since Wonderland RV opened shop in 2014, customisation and innovation are at the core of what we do. Our mission remained the same over the years: build bespoke caravans for caravanners who know what they want. We are attentive to their new ideas, we encourage our suppliers to push the boundaries of what’s possible, we listen to customer feedback and we test the caravans ourselves and always come back with a better way to do things.
