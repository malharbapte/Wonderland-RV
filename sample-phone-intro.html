Opening section — three different shapes

The last set was three versions of one idea: move the video up the column. These change the shape of the section instead — one layers the copy onto the film, one re-weights the type so the section opens with a voice, one puts the story on the page’s own cream. Each panel is a real 375px viewport. The video box is its placeholder at the exact size the clip fills. Desktop is untouched by all three.

A · Copy overlaps the film

The video runs edge to edge under the hero and the copy is lifted onto its lower edge as a white card — the same layering the hero and the Experience band already use. Gives the section a front-of-book feel, and the overlap buys back most of the height the video adds.

655px tall · film first · 68px shorter than today

B · Opens with a voice

Their first line is set as a lede at subheading size with the quote mark scaled up to match, the film follows that hook, and the rest of the story reads underneath at body size. Nothing is hidden and nothing is reordered away — the section just stops being one flat block of 91 words. It is the only one of the three that ends up taller than today, because the lede sets larger.

832px tall · film after the lede · 109px LONGER than today

C · The story in cream

The copy moves onto the same cream the travel-tips answer sits on, full bleed under the film. The two halves of the page then read as one system rather than loose text with a coloured panel appearing further down. The plainest of the three, and the most native to the page.
