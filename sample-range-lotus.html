What this sample needs before it can ship

  • 360 exterior. Your site has none — every "360" on it is an image-size suffix. A spin needs a 24–36 frame turntable or drone-orbit sequence per model, shot as a matched set. Until those exist the badge is a placeholder.
  • Popular layouts. The floorplan images on wonderlandrv.com.au load from a script, not the page, so they could not be read. Three per model, named, is what each placeholder is sized for.
  • Two of your four features barely differ. Water is 200L + 90L on three of four models and brakes are 12″ drum on three of four — so two of the four cells say the same thing three times. Battery and inverter separate the models cleanly. Noted below each variant.
