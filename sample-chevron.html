Chevron weight

Butt caps and a mitred point are the shape. The stroke width is the one number worth choosing — it is a token, so changing it changes every moving arrow on the site at once.

The live value is --chev-w: 2.6 in styles.css. Say a number and it moves — the range viewer, the Queen of Hearts deck, the Legendary Service arrows, the ambassador travel tips and the two build-page step arrows all read it. The build pages keep their own copy of the rule because they do not load styles.css.
