AM1806F-B2

The Amaroo AM1806F-B2 is the most compact and lightest family caravan layout we offer. It’s practical yet a spacious caravan, ideal for those seeking comfort but want to travel light.

It features a full-size queen bed in an east-west orientation, offering a luxurious pillow top mattress and reducing overall caravan length. Storage is ample with a 670L tunnel boot, 800L under-bed storage, and additional overhead and under-bunk options.

Designed for convenience and relaxation, the caravan includes an L-shape lounge that transforms into an extra bed, a straight door-side kitchen for engaging outdoor experiences, and a space-efficient open-plan bathroom, making it a top choice for a broad range of adventurers.
