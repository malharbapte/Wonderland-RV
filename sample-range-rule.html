The one decision in it

The two things the line separates are not the same width. The showcase bleeds the full window; the writing sits in the 16.24rem content column. So the line has to agree with one of them, and it reads differently depending which.

  • R1 Column — the line starts and stops where the writing does, so it reads as the top edge of the information block. Quieter, and it matches the hairlines already dividing the four features below it.
  • R2 Full bleed — the line runs the window, so it reads as a horizon under the vans rather than as a lid on the text. It also rhymes with the cream band at the foot, which already runs edge to edge, and the photo band above. Stronger, and more of a statement.
  • R3 is R1 at the heavier of the site's two hairline weights, in case 1px disappears at this scale on your screen.

My read: R2. The line's job here is to close off the showcase, and the showcase is full width — a line that stops short of the vans it is drawing a line under only half does it.
