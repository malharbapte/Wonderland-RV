Footer — condensing what follows the Subscribe CTA

All three are live — tap the headings in F2. Everything above the Subscribe button is the same in each, so only the link region differs. Corrected: an earlier version of this page drew the footer dark. It has no background of its own — it sits on the page's white, with black text and orange headings, as shown here. The Subscribe CTA now runs side to side like the other buttons.

Where the height is

The footer is 2053px on a phone. The five link groups below the Subscribe CTA are 982 of that — just under half — because a rule at the 520 breakpoint drops them from two columns to one, so all 22 links stack in a single file. The brand column above is 560 and the legal blocks 240; those are close to irreducible without cutting content. This is the piece worth condensing.

F1Two columns

Delete the 520 rule so the two-column grid holds all the way down. Nothing else changes — same groups, same links, same order, all still visible without a tap. The shortest route, and the only one that touches a single line of CSS.

—

F2Accordion

Five headings in a ruled stack; tap one and it opens. Uses the interaction already established twice on the homepage, in Build your dream van and Why choose us. By far the biggest saving, and it makes the footer scannable — but every link now costs a tap, and footers are where people hunt for one specific thing.

—

F3Two columns, locations inline

F1, plus the seven locations run as a wrapped row of names instead of a stacked list. They are the longest group and the shortest labels, so they are the one that wastes most as a column. Everything stays visible and nothing needs a tap.
