iPhone — Your dream van awaits

The desktop collage does not survive the width. Three ways to carry it instead.

What is wrongThe mosaic keeps its desktop shape at phone width, so the four photographs land at 141×118, 98×56, 98×56 and 140×118. The two middle ones are postage stamps — smaller than the icons two sections above them. The whole collage is 177 tall on a 390 screen.
The titleSits inside the mosaic on the desktop, overlaid on the photographs. Here it moves out to white above them, where it has room for its two lines.
The sourcesTwo are near-square (1628×1402) and two are landscape (1130×670), so a shared frame means cropping. All three below use 3:2 with object-position: 50% 50%, so what is lost comes off both edges evenly.

D1 — Stacked, full width

Four photographs at 3:2, one under the other, each the full measure. Every shot is 342 wide and properly readable. Longest of the three by a distance.
