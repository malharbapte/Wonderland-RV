Live, and running on its own. Leave it be and it advances every 3.4s; swipe it and it hands control back to you, then picks up again after a few seconds. It never reaches an end.

Now: the four shots are a 2×2 grid of tiles — everything visible at once, each one small, and nothing moving.

How the loop works

The four images are laid down three times, twelve slides in all, and the rail starts in the middle set. Each time it crosses a set boundary it moves a whole set width back or forward with the scroll behaviour off — the pixels either side of the seam are identical, so there is nothing to see. No clone appears twice on screen, and there is no rewind.

Swipe

It is a real scroller, not a transform track, so it snaps to centre under your finger. Touching it stops the timer; the timer restarts 6s after you let go.

Worth flagging

I cannot watch this run here — animations and transitions do not advance in the preview pane, and scroll events do not fire. The geometry is measured; the motion needs your eye on a real phone.
