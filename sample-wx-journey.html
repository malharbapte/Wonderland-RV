customisation journey

Your dream vision

Envision your dream caravan. What kind of trips would you take? How many companions will travel with you? What features and amenities are essential for your comfort? Get inspired by fellow Wonderlanders, caravan shows, and ideas shared on social media.

The fundamental decisions

Select the model that best matches your needs and preferences, and chose from over 60 pre-defined layouts for couples and family configurations. Add your specifications, length, chassis, suspension, wheels, insulation, and more.

Make it your own

Personalise your caravan with a wide range of upgrades. Choose from electrical system, to cladding, privacy options and appliances. You can also select your preferred interior finishes, fabrics, and colours. We’re excited to create a space that’s truly your own!

Designing your caravan

Our creative team will take your inputs to come up with the perfect van design. They’ll prepare floor plans and elevations, and make sure all your requests work perfectly together so there isn’t any surprises at a later stage.

Watch your van coming to life

Our unmatched craftsmanship uses a unique jigsaw wall construction method for strength, durability, and precision. We build only with premium materials from reputable suppliers. Watch your van come to life with progress updates and images!

Your travel journey begins here

After a thorough inspection, we will hand over the keys to your new caravan. In our comprehensive handover session, we will explain how to use and maintain it. It will come with a 3-year factory-backed warranty and premium after-sales service for your peace of mind and satisfaction.
