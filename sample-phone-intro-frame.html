We’re Jess and Doug, the family behind Sandy Van Travels, a full-time travelling family of five exploring Australia with our three children, Ellie and Arthur (our 7-year-old twins) and Harry (5).

After selling our home and most of what we owned in Brisbane, we custom built our first van in April 2024 and hit the road to create a life centred around family, adventure and experiences.

We now travel Australia full-time, working remotely, homeschooling our kids and documenting the journey through photography, video and honest storytelling.
