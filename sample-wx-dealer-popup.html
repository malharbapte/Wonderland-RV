Dealer pop-up on phone: a card, in three parts

The pop-up sits as a card on the dimmed page, like the other forms. The card is split top to bottom into three: the map (inset), the logo and name, then the details with the CTA at the foot. The cross and state name sit on the map. Arrows (or a swipe) go to the next state. Below: first the padding around the map and the content, then the two ways to show a state with two dealerships.
