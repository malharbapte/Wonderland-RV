What this is doing

  • The name is 0.60rem, which is 0.46 plus 30%. Tagline left, price right, everything else untouched.
  • The columns are 1fr auto 1fr, not two columns with the name shoved over. That is what makes it genuinely centred: the outer two are equal whatever the tagline and the price happen to measure, so the name sits on the column’s true midpoint and stays there when the name changes length from AMAROO to XTR.
  • No markup or script change. The name and the tagline live inside one wrapper because until now they belonged together on the left; display:contents dissolves that wrapper so both become items of the head’s own grid and can be placed in different columns.
  • C1 sits the three on one bottom line, so the name, the tagline and the price all finish together. C2 centres them on each other instead, which lifts the tagline and the price to the middle of the name and reads lighter but less anchored.
  • It costs 0.14rem of height, so the section goes from about 0.53 to 0.54 of the window width. Nothing else moves.

My read: C1. With the name this much bigger it wants a floor, and the shared bottom line gives the row one.
