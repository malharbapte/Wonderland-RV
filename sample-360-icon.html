A mark for the virtual tour

A is what is on the page: an ellipse round a dot, which is a globe — globes mean language and world, not “look around this room” — and at 0.62rem the ring and the dot close up into a blob with a hole. Six alternatives, each saying a different thing.

Made in Illustrator

H, I and J come out of assets/icons/360-icons.jsx — a 24×24 artboard, 0.96pt stroke, round cap and join. The set’s conventions are applied IN the file, so the export needs no correcting afterwards: viewBox 0 0 24 24, fill none, stroke 0.96. Each has an .ai source beside its .svg.
