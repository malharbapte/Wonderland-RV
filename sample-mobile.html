Mobile proposal — example before changes

Nothing on the live pages has been touched. This shows the two decisions you made: proportional type with a floor per tier, and swipeable carousels for Our Range, Why Choose Us and the dream shots. Section order stays exactly as desktop.

1 · Type — where the floors kick in

Each tier keeps scaling with the artboard until it hits its floor, then holds. Written as max(0.56rem, 28px), so one rule covers every width and no separate mobile scale is needed.

TierArtboardFloorFloor engages belowOn a 390px phone
Heading5628px1188px28px
Subheading3218px1336px18px
Body2816px1354px16px
Caption2013px1544px13px

Without floors, a 390px phone would render the heading at 9px and the caption at 3px — that is the flow you are seeing now.
